## 5. Geo-ads research refresh (verify-and-update for when the gate breaks)

Refreshed against current (June 2026) platform capabilities so `geo_ads_campaign_spec.md` is launch-ready when slots 4/6/7 clear. **No spend authorized — research only**, consistent with holding option (b) in reserve.

**Platform mechanics for a 50-mile radius from South Lake Tahoe:**

- **Meta (Instagram/Facebook) — best fit for true radius targeting.** Meta Ads Manager supports drop-a-pin + radius from **1 to 50 miles**, set via the distance slider. A 50-mile radius from South Lake Tahoe is at the *top* of Meta's allowed range and is the correct Phase-1 cap (it covers South Lake, Truckee, Tahoe City, Incline Village, and reaches toward Reno/Carson). Use the **"People living in this location"** relationship for creator recruitment (we want resident creatives, not tourists). Minimum radius isn't a constraint here; the 50mi cap is.
- **TikTok — no native drop-pin + radius.** As of 2026, TikTok still does **not** offer Facebook-style pin+radius; you approximate a 50-mile ring with **Zip Code and City/Town targeting**. For the Tahoe basin that means a Zip-code list (96150 South Lake Tahoe, 96161 Truckee, 96145 Tahoe City, 89451 Incline Village, plus Reno/Carson zips if widening). Build the spec as a zip-list, not a radius, for TikTok.
- **Budget reality for a bootstrapped test.** TikTok minimums: **$20/day ad-group, $50/day campaign**; a meaningful first test wants **$500–$1,000** total. This validates the prior spec's $300–500 figure as the floor, not the comfortable test size — flag to Finance that a real TikTok test reads closer to $500–$1,000.
- **TikTok new-advertiser matched-credit incentive (NEW, worth capturing):** TikTok's 2026 program matches first-30-day spend dollar-for-dollar up to **$6,000** across a 5-tier menu ($200/$500/$1k/$3k/$6k). **Material for Shutter:** when (b) eventually fires, choosing the $500 tier effectively doubles a $500 test to $1,000 of delivery. Routed to Finance as a budget-efficiency note for the eventual launch — do NOT chase it now (it starts a 30-day clock and we're density-gated).
- **Creative QA dependency unchanged:** any paid creative featuring a creator requires the SB-478 fee-disclosure verification (Legal slot 6) AND, if it features an identifiable creator, the DM-screenshot consent OR in-app `consent_log` checkbox (Legal May 26). Organic-only spotlights remain covered by implicit consent.
