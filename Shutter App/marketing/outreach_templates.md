## Goal
Get to 25 real creators on the Shutter map. Current: 7 seeded (zero real). Gap: **18 real signups**.

## Why outreach (not just ads)
At <25 creators, density is too thin for ad spend to convert. Every ad click will land on a near-empty map. Warm 1:1 outreach is the only thing that moves the needle until day 60. Ads stage now, launch when density is proof-able.

## Target personas (in priority order)
1. **Snowboard / ski filmers** already shooting in Tahoe — biggest overlap with Patrick's network, highest trust, likely to book peer gigs. Target: 8 signups.
2. **Ski / snowboard photographers** — 1-day a week is enough; they value the Available-Now signal. Target: 4 signups.
3. **FAA Part 107 drone operators** — scarce, high demand from creators + brands. Target: 3 signups.
4. **Models & athletes** (skiers, snowboarders, bikers) with public social presence — crucial for visual content that seeds the map. Target: 3 signups.

## General outreach principles
- **Send from Patrick's personal handle.** Not an anonymous brand account. This is a founder-to-creator cold DM — authenticity is the moat.
- **Lead with utility, not product.** "I built a map that shows who's shooting in Tahoe right now" > "Join our marketplace."
- **Offer something concrete.** First week = "Be one of the first 10 real creators — you'll be featured in my launch post." Later = "I'll boost your first gig post for free when Gig Feed ships."
- **No bulk sending.** One personalized DM per creator per day max, from the owner's phone. Platforms (IG, TikTok) rate-limit and shadow-ban mass-DM patterns.
- **Track in a sheet.** Name, handle, platform, sent date, response, signup y/n, notes. Marketing Agent will append signup status to weekly reports.
- **Follow up once** after 5 days if no reply; never more than twice.
